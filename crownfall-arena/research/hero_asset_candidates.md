## Rejected
These are rejected for licence reasons:
- Constantine colossal hand (Scan the World): BY-NC-SA.
- Rodin "Hand of God": BY-SA.
- Smithsonian hominin skulls: not downloadable.
- Hyperdense ruins pack: AI-generated, provenance unclear.
- Global Digital Heritage scans: NC.

Sketchfab downloads need a free account (the user's own). Decimate anything over 1M tris for VR and bake detail to
normal maps.
