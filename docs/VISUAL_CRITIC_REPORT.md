# Independent Visual Critic Report

Date: 2026-08-04

Final verdict: **ACCEPT**

## Review set

- Six exact title-screen viewports: 1920×1080, 1440×900, 1366×768, 1024×768, 390×844, and 360×800
- Campaign War Table at 1440×900
- Mission I battle at 1440×900
- Menu-to-mission MP4 supplied as motion evidence

## Initial rejection

The first independent review accepted the menu direction but rejected the complete slice for seven blocking issues:

1. Wall-dominated, placeholder-looking battle environment.
2. Flat board, wall, and stone response.
3. Tiny low-contrast difficulty and battle labels.
4. A generic War Table made from empty rectangular dashboard cards.
5. Muddy battle silhouettes.
6. Mobile difficulty cards pushed the CTA below the fold and the mode rail lacked a clear continuation cue.
7. Motion clipping and camera obstruction were not independently verified.

## Remediation review

| Area | Final status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile CTA, navigation, and density | Resolved | The chapter CTA is visible at 390×844 and 360×800. The 2×2 difficulty grid fits without panel overflow; 390 px also shows Settings and a partial next mode. |
| War Table presentation | Resolved | The empty primary card is now a campaign map with routes, nodes, labels, mission context, Cael art, commander context, and campaign state. |
| Label contrast | Resolved | Critical navigation, CTA, objective, heading, and battle HUD labels remain readable against their surfaces. |
| Battle environment and readability | Resolved | A closer camera, broken circular stone setting, clear board state, character badges/glows, Cael portrait, and separated HUD make the encounter legible. The former curtain-wall impression is gone. |
| Text and UI overlap | Pass | The title, tableau, navigation, artefact panel, objective HUD, character strip, and controls hold separate safe regions across the supplied viewports. |
| Bloom and grading | Pass | Highlights are controlled; neither the board nor typography is buried under global bloom or blur. |
| Character presence | Pass | Cael is visible as a named person in the title witness, portrait-led story, dossier, battle strip, and campaign outcomes. |

## Final verdict

> **ACCEPTED.** Final responsive menu, campaign hub, and battle captures clear all previously identified visual blockers. Remaining issues are polish debt involving narrow-screen microcopy, navigation affordance, subtle map labels, and procedural environment repetition; none prevents Phase 1 approval.

The work extracts the principles of premium tactical RPG and cinematic strategy interfaces—clear hierarchy, diegetic campaign artefacts, character-led state, restrained grading, and tactical legibility—without copying a specific copyrighted layout or asset.

## Residual non-blocking debt

- At 360 px, difficulty descriptions are tightly wrapped and mode-rail continuation could be clearer.
- Some difficulty-card and status microcopy remains small or crowded.
- White board tiles still dominate the battle frame; surrounding stone and loose-rock materials show procedural repetition.
- Hub map labels are subtle at native size.
- The critic could not independently decode the MP4, so motion/camera clipping remains an evidence caveat. The primary QA pass separately decoded the file and visually inspected extracted menu, flow, and battle frames.
