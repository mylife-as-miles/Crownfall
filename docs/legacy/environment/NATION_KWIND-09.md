# Kwind-09 — design sheet: the charter yard

*The Kwind Zaibatsu — a chartered enclave in a stratified corporate metropolis, never a neon
village.* First-pass layout (`status: "first-pass"`). Data:
`shared/legacy/environment/blueprint/nations/kwind-09.ts`. Maps: `maps/kwind-09-*.png`. All names
are working placeholders.

## Terrain logic — engineered ground, altitude as standing

No natural ground survives inside the enclave. A reclaimed foundry quay at **+3 m** carries the
lower yard; a structural **podium deck at +11 m** carries the house's own buildings behind an 8 m
retaining face, so standing reads as altitude at a glance. The deck is reached three ways: the
Deck Stair along the podium face to a gated lodge, a caged freight lift, and a 43 m service ramp.
The quay wall drops 7.5 m to an old foundry harbour basin (water at −1.2 m) to the south, with a
landing stair to a single pontoon. West of the arterial lie industrial flats at about +2 m with slag
heaps and the holding's allotments; to the east a neighbouring combine's container yard; to the
north the expressway on 20 m piers with an off-ramp down to the arterial at the checkpoint.

## Organisation — deck over yard

- **A Charter Deck (+11 m).** The charter hall — the foundry company's old hall in soot-black brick
  under a dark-tiled roof with upturned curved eaves, a steel ledger tower bolted to its flank
  (31 m above the floor), an old brick flue, a portico carrying the banner's new cyan glass sign,
  two upper bays shuttered; the five-storey household block behind lattice screens with a planted
  roof; the canteen; the glass gate lodge with its turnstile; the honour terrace with eight dark
  lamp plinths and a blank bronze ledger plate; the annex frame (three storeys of bare steel,
  scaffold, idle tower crane).
- **B Muster Yard (+3 m).** A painted loading yard beneath the podium wall: a steel-edged muster
  dock with two standards, eight painted file marks a–h and the Pawn's mark on axis, the drill
  square, a container armoury and a narrow three-storey levy dormitory. The deck's rail looks down
  on all of it.
- **C Charter Street Blocks.** Two rows between Charter Street and the quay, split by Ledger Lane
  and Tank Alley with back courts between: a tiled heritage supervisor's house, gable-fronted brick
  rows with a lantern-mender's blade sign, a six-storey gallery block with a roof tank, a leaning
  old tenement propped with steel, stacked cargo containers by the damp quay, a quay tenement, a
  clean founding prefab, a narrow tiled house, and a burnt store with shacks on its roof. A lamp
  shrine, a standpipe with a queue, laundry across the courts.
- **D Works and Stores.** A sawtooth fabrication shed with a welding bay under the street canopy, the
  freight lift on the podium face, a vaulted ration depot, a riveted water tower on a trestle, a
  substation, and a brick quay warehouse; a quay crane on rails and a quay rail line.
- **E Checkpoint and Signal Office.** The only road entrance: a concrete checkpoint under a sign
  gantry carrying the banner's mark beside the Zaibatsu's, barrier arms and a glass booth; the
  signal office with a lamp mast, a pneumatic tube from the checkpoint and a public ledger board of
  mechanical flaps over the door; the courier bay.
- **F Ledger Vault.** The War Record: a concrete vault under a green copper barrel roof with an
  index clock, joined to the charter hall by a glazed skybridge; the previous charter holder's
  ledgers fill the lower drawers, the banner's record begins in one cabinet by the door.
- **G Outer Territory.** The harbour and the far quay with the relay mast, the allotments, fenced
  waste lots, the neighbour's container yard, the toll gantry on the expressway.

## Dominant shapes and skyline

Stacked and layered: the podium's horizontal wall with the tiled charter hall and the steel ledger
tower rising above it, flat-roofed blocks crowded with tanks and vents, the sawtooth shed, the
water tower, cranes. Behind, the expressway runs across the whole view on its piers with traffic
lights moving. Beyond: the allied Great House's exchange spire of glass and bronze lit cyan (20°,
3.8 km), the capital's wall of corporate towers stacked over the old city, crowns lost in smog
(34°, 14 km, always "the capital"), an arcology on legs above the rail yards (70°, 7 km), the
western cooling towers' steam (280°, 2.6 km) and the gantry cranes of the old foundry docks across
the water.

## Construction traditions

`kw-charter-heritage` (the charter hall, the vault, the supervisor's and narrow houses, brick rows),
`kw-ferroconcrete` (the deck, blocks, dormitory, signal office, checkpoint, substation),
`kw-sheet-industrial` (sheds, depot, water tower, containers, shacks). Parameters in
`STYLE_GUIDE.md` §3 — these need their own kit kinds; Kwind-09 must never fall back to timber
jetties or rubble cottages.

## Life

Sentries on the north and west barrier walks, guards at the barrier arms and the deck gate, a
fabricator at the welding bay and power hammer, a Zaibatsu courier coming down the arterial to the
signal office, the levy drilling in the yard, a signal clerk at the ledger board, a ledger clerk
crossing from the vault to the hall, a shift of five crossing from the blocks to the shed, a
laundress, a noodle stall keeper at the shift change, a resident carrying water from the standpipe,
children in the back court, a crane hand, a hand-cart carter, allotment keepers.

## Atmosphere

Last light of the day shift: a smog-red sun low in the west (bearing 262°, elevation 5°), signage
coming on, rain on the concrete. Hierarchy by light: cyan glass and clean steel on the deck, sodium
and fluorescent in the lower yard, furnace orange in the works. Signage is sparse and functional —
no neon clutter; the towers beyond carry the brightest light.
