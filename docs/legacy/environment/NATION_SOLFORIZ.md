# Solforiz — design sheet: the terraced holding

*The Scorched Caliphate of Solforiz — sulfur deserts and fortified settlements.* First-pass layout
(`status: "first-pass"`), validated to the same rules as Korvenia. Data:
`shared/legacy/environment/blueprint/nations/solforiz.ts` (authored in a local frame rotated 45°:
local +Z runs downhill to the south-east). Maps: `maps/solforiz-*.png`. All names are working
placeholders; no real-world religious titles, scriptures or practices are depicted.

## Terrain logic — an engineered cascade

An escarpment falls from a plateau (+34 m) in the north-west to a mineral basin (−13 m) in the
south-east. The holding is four level terraces held by thick retaining walls — **manor terrace
+14 m, houses and parade court +8 m, market and works +2 m, gate court −3 m** — joined by stairs on
one axial spine. A dry wadi runs past the south-west wall; a stone rill carries the manor's cistern
water down the spine, dropping in small cascades at each terrace, to a stepped reservoir and the
oasis gardens outside the walls. Strata show as ridged bands on the cliff; sulfur domes, a salt dome
and a yellow-green mineral pool break the basin crust.

## Organisation — one spine, shade and water

- **A Manor Terrace (+14 m).** The Banner Hall (a 20 × 11 m hall behind a tall iwan portal, a
  green-glazed dome and a great windcatcher rising 22.5 m above the floor — the tallest thing in the
  holding); the household wing round a shaded arcaded court with its own small windcatcher; a tall
  blank manor gate at the head of the Manor Stair; the unfinished new cistern house (walls to 3 m,
  scaffold); the Court of Honours with eight empty lamp niches in the arcade piers before the iwan.
- **B Parade Court (+8 m).** A walled sand court with a stepped dais, file posts and the Pawn's
  marked stone, an armoury and a one-room levy house.
- **C Shade Lane Quarter (+8 m).** Courtyard houses that turn blank walls to a shaded blind lane:
  a merchant's house with a windcatcher and a fountain court, weavers', potters' and a water-seller's
  houses, an old low court house, a labourers' tenement, a damaged house by the wall, a house sealed
  with the previous holders' mark.
- **D Market and Works Terrace (+2 m).** The covered market street running straight down the spine,
  three beehive grain domes sealed with the steward's mark, a storehouse, the sulfur works with its
  tall banded flues, the smithy, and the caravan court inside the gate.
- **E Basin Gate (−3 m).** Gate towers on the lower wall where the causeway arrives, the gate
  square, and the herald's signal tower with its post court and signal mirror.
- **F War Record.** A domed archive on the manor court's east side joined to the hall by its arcade:
  cool, dim, lined with niches; the previous holders' tablets below, the banner's record begun on a
  single shelf by the door.
- **G Outer Territory.** The reservoir and oasis gardens with a pump house, the causeway across the
  flats with signal and toll towers, crust diggers on the basin, the plateau with the spring house
  that feeds the cistern and the crown aqueduct striding along the rim.

## Dominant shapes and skyline

Horizontal masses stepped like a fortified cascade, cut by verticals: the great windcatcher, the
glazed dome, the herald's signal tower, the works flues. Battered walls, crenellated parapets, deep
shadowed arcades and covered streets read as dark slots in warm stone. Beyond: mineral spires and
buttes in the basin, the refinery's flare stacks and smoke trail (102°, 9 km), the allied Great
House's walled palace on a mesa across the basin (160°, 5.5 km), the caravan town (140°, 6.5 km),
the capital's monumental terraces and refinery towers hazed gold (135°, 28 km, "the capital").

## Construction traditions

`sf-cut-sandstone` (manor, archive, gates, towers), `sf-courtyard-house` (dwellings, market,
granaries), `sf-works` (industry). Parameters in `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §3. The green-glazed dome tile is
the holding's one luxury; repairs use plain fired clay.

## Life

Sentries on the lower wall and the rim wall, gate guards, the smith at the works forge, a courier
riding in along the causeway, the levy drilling on the parade sand, the herald at the post court,
the archive scribe crossing the court, a mason at the cistern house, a water-seller carrying jars up
the spine, works hands carrying sulfur to the market, children in Shade Lane, gardeners opening the
oasis channels, crust crews on the basin, a caravan crossing the flats.

## Atmosphere

Late afternoon in sulfur haze: a low, heavy sun (bearing 232°, elevation 19°), the basin glowing
below the terraces. Warm sandstone, sulfur yellow and green glaze; shade is the luxury, so arcades,
covered streets and court interiors stay deep and cool. Furnaces and storm lanterns as accents.
